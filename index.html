A bit about me

01 / About

I started scripting because a game I loved kept breaking, and I wanted to understand why instead of just complaining in the comments.

So I opened Studio, read a lot of terrible code, wrote a lot more of it myself, and slowly figured things out. Four years later I am the person other developers message when their project works fine in a private test and then falls over the moment real players show up.

Most of my work is structural. I take something tangled, rebuild the core into clean modules, and hand it back with comments so anyone on your team can pick it up without needing me in the call.

I also try to make the process pleasant. No jargon walls, no ghosting, no pretending a two week job takes two months. If something is a bad idea I will say so kindly, and if you are new to hiring a scripter I will walk you through it.

02 / Process

01

You say hi

Send me the idea in whatever shape it is in. A document, a video, or one sentence in a Discord message all work. I ask a few friendly questions to make sure we picture the same thing.

05 / FAQ

What does it cost +

It depends on what you need, so I quote each job on its own. A single small system is a modest one off fee, a full framework is a bigger conversation. I happily take payment in Robux through a group payout or a gamepass, or in USD if that is easier for you, and I will tell you the exact number before any work begins so there are no surprises.

How long will it take +

A single system is usually a few days. Larger projects get split into stages so you always have something to look at while we go. I would rather give you an honest date and meet it than promise a weekend and leave you waiting.

I am new to this, is that ok +

Completely. Plenty of people message me with just an idea and no plan yet. I will explain things in normal language, help you shape the scope, and tell you if something can be done in a simpler or cheaper way. Asking questions is never annoying.

Can you join a team that already exists +

Yes, and I enjoy it. I am comfortable with Rojo and Git, code reviews, and following whatever style your team already uses. I will fit into your project rather than asking you to rebuild around me.

What happens after you deliver +

If something I wrote breaks, I fix it for free, with no deadline on that. New features count as new work. If you would like me around for regular updates, we can set up a simple monthly arrangement.

Do I get the source code +

Always, and it is yours to keep. Full commented source plus a short guide on how the pieces fit together, so you or anyone you hire later can carry on without me.
