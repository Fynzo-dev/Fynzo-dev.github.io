Who I am

01 / About

I got into scripting because a game I played kept dropping to nine frames every time a round started, and nobody could tell me why.

Turned out the answer was boring: bad loops, unmanaged connections, remotes firing every frame. I fixed it, then kept fixing things for other people, and somewhere along the way it became the job.

Today I mostly get called in for the structural stuff. Someone has a game that works fine with five testers and falls apart with fifty. I come in, read the mess, rewrite the core into something modular, and hand it back documented so the next person is not cursing my name.

I care about two things: code you can still read in six months, and gameplay that feels instant on a phone from 2019.

◈

Modular cores

Object oriented ModuleScripts with clean boundaries. Swap a feature out without the whole place catching fire.
⚡

Server authority

Remotes validated, rates limited, state owned by the server. Exploiters get a very short and boring session.
◐

Data that survives

Session locked datastores with retries, versioning and rollback. Player progress does not vanish on a bad shutdown.
◇

UI with weight

Client controllers, spring based motion, real feedback on every press. Menus that feel expensive to touch.

04 / FAQ

What does a job cost +

Depends entirely on scope. Small standalone systems land in the low double digits USD, full frameworks are a different conversation. Tell me what you need and I give you a flat number before anything starts, so there are no surprises halfway through.

How fast do you deliver +

A single system is usually a few days. Larger builds get split into milestones so you always have something playable in hand. I would rather quote honestly and hit the date than promise a weekend and disappear.

Do you work inside a team +

Yes, and I prefer it. I am comfortable with Rojo and Git, code reviews, and matching whatever style guide your team already uses. I do not need to rewrite your project to work on it.

What about after delivery +

Bugs in what I wrote get fixed free, no time limit on that. New features are new work. If you want me on retainer for ongoing updates that is easy to arrange.
